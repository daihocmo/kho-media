### Các Podcast về tin tức

1. The New York Times: Known for its high-quality journalism and in-depth reporting, The New York Times covers a wide range of topics from news and politics to culture and opinion pieces.
2. The Guardian: A British newspaper that offers a mix of news, opinion, and features on various topics. The Guardian is known for its progressive stance on social issues.
3. The Washington Post: Another reputable newspaper with a focus on American politics and current events. It also covers a wide range of other topics, including international news and culture.
4. The Wall Street Journal: A business-focused newspaper that covers financial news, politics, and international affairs. The Wall Street Journal is known for its coverage of economic issues and business trends.
5. The Economist: A weekly publication that covers global news, politics, business, and finance. The Economist is known for its in-depth analysis and commentary on international affairs.
6. BBC News: While not a newspaper, the BBC News website offers a wealth of articles on current events, politics, culture, and more. It provides a good mix of UK and international news.
7. USA Today: A widely circulated American newspaper that covers a broad range of topics, including news, sports, entertainment, and lifestyle.
8. The Times (UK): A British newspaper known for its comprehensive coverage of news, politics, and culture. It offers a mix of in-depth analysis and opinion pieces.

### Một danh sách khác

- [**Making Sense with Sam Harris**](https://open.spotify.com/show/6GSnbFz5qzNuKnLmGzQbPc) ([alternative](https://www.samharris.org/podcasts)) - Discourse rõ ràng, analytical. Giúp luyện nghe cách xây dựng argument dài, mạch lạc.

- [**The Tim Ferriss Show**](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-tim-ferriss-show/id863897795) ([alternative](https://tim.blog/podcast)) - Interview sâu, nhiều storytelling cá nhân. Học được cách người Mỹ nói về kinh nghiệm sống.

- [**Armchair Expert**](https://open.spotify.com/show/6kAsbP8pxwaU2kPibKTuHE) ([alternative](https://armchairexpertpod.com)) - Casual, nhiều interruption, laughter, meta-commentary. Rất tốt để nghe conversational overlap tự nhiên.

- [**This American Life**](https://open.spotify.com/show/41zWZdWCpVQrKj7ykQnXRc) ([alternative](https://www.thisamericanlife.org)) - Narrative kiểu Mỹ, storytelling có cấu trúc nhưng vẫn gần gũi. Giúp hiểu cultural framing.

- [**The Daily**](https://open.spotify.com/show/3IM0lmZxpFAY7CwMuv9H4g) ([alternative](https://www.nytimes.com/column/the-daily)) - News-based nhưng conversational, không formal như báo viết. Tốt để nghe cách hỏi–đáp thực tế.

- [**Stuff You Should Know**](https://open.spotify.com/show/0ofXAdFIQQRsCYj9754UFx) ([alternative](https://stuffyoushouldknow.com/)) - Hai host nói chuyện tự nhiên, nhiều filler, tangent. Rất sát spoken American rhythm.

- [**SmartLess**](https://open.spotify.com/show/0Yzd0g8NYmn27k2HFNplv7) ([alternative](https://www.smartless.com)) - Humor, improv nhẹ, banter liên tục. Giúp hiểu comedic timing và social cues.

- [**The Ezra Klein Show**](https://open.spotify.com/show/3oB5noYIwEB2dMAREj2F7S) ([alternative](https://www.nytimes.com/column/ezra-klein-podcast)) - Structured thinking + conversational delivery. Luyện khả năng theo dõi idea chain dài.

- [**Freakonomics Radio**](https://open.spotify.com/show/6z4NLXyHPga1UmSJsPK7G1) ([alternative](https://freakonomics.com/)) - Story-driven analysis, clear narrative reasoning.

- [**Huberman Lab**](https://open.spotify.com/show/79CkJF3UJTHFV8Dse3Oy0P) ([alternative](https://www.hubermanlab.com/podcast)) - Giải thích khoa học bằng spoken English tương đối tự nhiên, không hoàn toàn academic.

- [**The Moth**](https://open.spotify.com/show/2JqG6b6Z5K3m0w7K1v5Z6Z) ([alternative](https://themoth.org/listen)) - Storytelling live, không script cứng.

- [**Call Her Daddy**](https://open.spotify.com/show/0e3hcfx1W0w4Q6j3v1Y8vQ) ([alternative](https://www.callherdaddy.com)) - Modern informal American female speech, slang-heavy, cultural references mạnh.
